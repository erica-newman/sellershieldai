03 — Lead Intelligence
AI Lead Scoring

Hot vs. Cold — What Happens Next

Hot Lead
High Intent
Fast timeline · large budget · ready to act
  • 1Agent detects urgency signals ("buy ASAP", large budget)
  • 2Lead pushed to CRM immediately with full contact data
  • 3WhatsApp sequence activated for deep nurture
  • 4Director's WhatsApp link shared for direct connect
  • 5CRM notes updated after every conversation turn
Example trigger: "How soon? → ASAP" + "Budget? → $10M" → Director link sent immediately
Cold Lead
Low Intent
No timeline · vague signals · not yet ready
  • 1Agent answers questions politely — no aggressive push
  • 2Conversation ends gracefully — no sales rep time wasted
  • 3Lead tagged as cold in CRM for future re-targeting
  • 4Available for lookalike audience building
  • 5Re-entered into funnel if behavior changes
Key benefit: Sales reps only touch hot leads — no more sifting through hundreds of dead calls
